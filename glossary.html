Solar glossary

Solar has its own vocabulary. Here are the key terms explained in plain English — each one linked to the calculator or guide where it matters most.

ABCDGHIKMNOPSTVW

A

Amp-hour (Ah)

A measure of battery capacity: the current a battery can deliver for one hour. Multiply by voltage to get watt-hours of stored energy.

Battery Ah to kWh converter →

Ampacity

The maximum current a cable can safely carry before it overheats. Choosing a conductor means keeping the current below its ampacity and within the voltage-drop limit.

Cable size calculator →

Array

A group of solar panels wired together. The array's voltage and current depend on how many panels are in series and how many strings are in parallel.

Series & parallel calculator →

Azimuth

The compass direction a panel faces. In the northern hemisphere, true south usually captures the most energy over the year.

Panel tilt & angle calculator →

B

Bypass diode

A diode built into a panel that lets current flow around a shaded or faulty section, reducing the energy lost when part of a panel is in shade.

Shading loss calculator →

C

Charge controller

The device between the panels and battery that regulates charging and protects the battery. Comes in two types, PWM and MPPT.

MPPT vs PWM charge controllers →

Clipping

The energy lost when a solar array briefly produces more power than the inverter's AC rating can pass, so the inverter caps its output. A little clipping is normal in an oversized array.

DC to AC ratio calculator →

D

DC/AC ratio

The array's DC capacity divided by the inverter's AC rating (also called the inverter loading ratio). Most systems sit around 1.1 to 1.3.

DC to AC ratio calculator →

Degradation

The slow, permanent decline in a panel's output over the years, usually around 0.5% per year, so a panel still makes most of its rated power after 25 years.

Panel degradation calculator →

Depth of discharge (DoD)

The share of a battery's capacity you can safely use before recharging, roughly 50% for lead-acid and 80 to 90% for lithium. Deeper discharges shorten battery life.

Battery Ah to kWh converter →

Derating

Reducing a component's rated value to allow for real conditions, most often the drop in panel output as cell temperature rises above 25 degrees C.

Temperature derating calculator →

G

Grid-tied

A solar system connected to the utility grid, with no battery, that feeds surplus energy back to the grid and draws from it when the sun is down.

Grid-tied vs off-grid vs hybrid →

H

Hybrid system

A system that combines grid connection with a battery, so it can store energy, use the grid as backup, and keep running during outages.

Grid-tied vs off-grid vs hybrid →

I

Imp

The current a panel produces at its maximum power point under standard test conditions. Strings in parallel add their Imp values.

Series & parallel calculator →

Inverter

The device that converts the panels' and battery's DC electricity into the AC electricity used by appliances and the grid.

Inverter size calculator →

Irradiance

The power of sunlight hitting a surface, measured in watts per square metre. The standard 'full sun' benchmark is 1000 W/m2.

Peak sun hours by location →

Isc

A panel's short-circuit current, its highest possible current, used for sizing fuses and checking equipment current limits.

Series & parallel calculator →

K

kWh (kilowatt-hour)

A unit of energy: one kilowatt used for one hour. Your electricity bill, daily use, and battery storage are all measured in kWh.

Battery Ah to kWh converter →

kWp (kilowatt-peak)

A solar array's rated power under standard test conditions, i.e. the sum of the panels' peak wattages. Real output is usually a bit lower.

Solar panel calculator →

M

MPPT

Maximum Power Point Tracking, a smart charge controller that runs panels at their most productive voltage and converts the surplus into extra current, harvesting 15 to 30% more than PWM.

MPPT vs PWM charge controllers →

N

Net metering

A billing arrangement where the surplus energy your solar exports to the grid is credited against the energy you later import.

Solar bill savings calculator →

O

Off-grid

A standalone solar system with batteries and no grid connection, which must be sized to cover the load through the night and cloudy days.

Off-grid system wizard →

P

Parallel

Wiring strings side by side so their currents add up while the voltage stays the same. Used to increase array current.

Series & parallel calculator →

Peak sun hours (PSH)

The number of hours per day that sunlight would need to shine at full 1000 W/m2 intensity to deliver the day's total energy. Numerically equal to daily kWh/m2.

Peak sun hours by location →

Performance ratio (PR)

The fraction of an array's theoretical output that actually reaches the meter after heat, wiring, dust, and inverter losses, typically 0.75 to 0.85.

Energy production calculator →

PWM

Pulse-Width Modulation, a simple, cheap charge controller that connects the panel almost directly to the battery, wasting any panel voltage above the battery's level.

MPPT vs PWM charge controllers →

S

Series

Wiring panels end to end so their voltages add up while the current stays the same. Used to reach the inverter's operating voltage.

Series & parallel calculator →

Shading loss

The energy an array loses when part of it is shaded. Series-wired panels suffer more because one shaded panel drags down the whole string.

Shading loss calculator →

STC

Standard Test Conditions, the lab benchmark panels are rated at: 1000 W/m2 irradiance, 25 degrees C cell temperature, and a set air mass. Real conditions give lower output.

Temperature derating calculator →

String

A set of panels wired in series. The string voltage is the panel voltage times the number of panels, and must fit the inverter's voltage window.

String sizing calculator →

T

Temperature coefficient

How much a panel's voltage or power changes per degree of temperature. Panels lose power as they heat up and gain voltage as they cool.

Temperature derating calculator →

Tilt angle

The angle a panel is tilted from horizontal. The best fixed tilt is close to your latitude, adjusted for whether you want summer or winter output.

What angle should solar panels be →

V

Vmp

The voltage a panel produces at its maximum power point. Panels in series add their Vmp to set the string's operating voltage.

Series & parallel calculator →

Voc

A panel's open-circuit voltage, its highest voltage with no load, which rises in cold weather. A string's cold Voc must stay below the inverter's maximum input voltage.

String sizing calculator →

Voltage drop

The loss of voltage along a cable due to its resistance. Kept low (usually under 3%) by choosing a thick enough conductor for the current and distance.

Voltage drop calculator →

W

Watt-peak (Wp)

A single panel's rated power under standard test conditions. A 450 Wp panel produces 450 watts at full sun in the lab.

Solar panel calculator →
