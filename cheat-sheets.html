Wire size & ampacity (copper)

Approximate current a copper conductor can carry. Always confirm against your local electrical code, the cable's insulation rating, and derating for temperature and grouping.

System voltage guide (12 / 24 / 48 V)

Higher system voltage means lower current for the same power — thinner cables and less voltage drop. Bigger systems use higher voltage.

Battery voltageTypical system sizeBest for
12 VUp to ~1–1.5 kWRVs, boats, sheds, small setups, short runs
24 V~1.5–3 kWMedium off-grid homes and cabins
48 VAbove ~3 kWLarger homes and off-grid; most modern battery systems

Battery depth of discharge & life

How deep you can safely discharge each battery type, and roughly how many cycles it lasts. Staying within the recommended DoD extends battery life.

Battery typeRecommended DoDTypical cycle life
Lead-acid (flooded)~50%500 – 1,000 cycles
Lead-acid (AGM / gel)~50–60%600 – 1,200 cycles
Lithium (LiFePO4)~80–90%3,000 – 6,000+ cycles
