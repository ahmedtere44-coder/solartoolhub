Solar panels in Morocco

Morocco is a North-African solar leader — home to the giant Noor Ouarzazate complex — with strong sunshine and an ambitious national renewables target. For homes and businesses, rooftop solar is an increasingly sensible investment.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Morocco

Morocco gets excellent sun — roughly 5 to 6.5 peak sun hours. The south and the pre-Sahara are highest; the Atlantic coast a little lower.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Ouarzazate & the pre-Sahara~6.0 – 6.5
Marrakesh & the interior~5.5 – 6.0
Fes, Meknes & central plains~5.2 – 5.7
Casablanca & Atlantic coast~5.0 – 5.5
Rif & northern mountains~4.8 – 5.3

Use your local figure in the solar panel calculator and the energy production calculator. Background: peak sun hours by location.

⚡ Electricity prices in Morocco

Residential electricity (ONEE and local distributors) is billed on rising slabs, roughly MAD 0.90 to MAD 1.50+ per kWh as consumption rises. Higher-use households sit in the upper slabs, where solar self-generation saves the most.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Morocco has a strong national renewable-energy programme, but low-voltage residential net metering has been more limited than the medium/high-voltage schemes used by industry. Rules have been evolving, so many homes size for self-consumption. Confirm the current low-voltage connection and net-metering rules with ONEE or your local distributor.

💡 Local insight: Morocco

Morocco has world-class sun and national momentum behind renewables, so the resource is not the issue — the main thing to check locally is the residential net-metering and grid-connection rules, which have lagged the big utility-scale projects. Where full export isn't allowed yet, sizing for daytime self-consumption still gives a solid return given the strong sun.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Morocco get?

About 5 to 6.5. The south and pre-Sahara (Ouarzazate) are highest at ~6–6.5, while the Atlantic coast is around 5–5.5.

Is solar worth it in Morocco?

Yes for the sun resource, especially for higher-consumption homes. Payback is commonly around 6–9 years; check local net-metering rules to maximise the return.

Does Morocco have net metering?

National renewables policy is strong, but low-voltage residential net metering has historically been more limited than industrial schemes. Confirm current rules with ONEE or your distributor.

How much does solar cost in Morocco?

Roughly MAD 40,000–65,000 for a typical 5 kW home system. Get local quotes.

Note: Moroccan electricity tariffs, net-metering rules and prices change over time. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with ONEE / your distributor and a local installer.
