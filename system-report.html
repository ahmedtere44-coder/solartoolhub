How this report is built

This page ties the core off-grid calculations together so you can see a whole system from one set of inputs. The solar array is sized so it replaces your daily energy use after real-world losses: array kW = daily kWh ÷ (peak sun hours × efficiency). The battery bank is sized to cover your usage across the autonomy days you choose, divided by the usable depth of discharge. The inverter is suggested from the array size, and the yearly output is the array's realistic annual generation.

Each figure is a sensible first pass. To refine any part, jump to its dedicated tool: the panel calculator, battery calculator, days of autonomy calculator, inverter calculator, and energy production calculator. For the bigger picture, see the system sizing guide.
