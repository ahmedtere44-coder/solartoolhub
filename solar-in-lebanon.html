Solar panels in Lebanon

Lebanon's electricity crisis has made solar not a luxury but a necessity. With state power available only a few hours a day in many areas and private generators extremely expensive, rooftop solar-plus-battery has spread rapidly across the country.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Lebanon

Lebanon has a sunny Mediterranean climate — roughly 4.8 to 5.6 peak sun hours — highest on the coast and in the Bekaa Valley, a little lower in the mountains.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Bekaa Valley~5.2 – 5.6
Coast (Beirut, Tripoli)~5.0 – 5.5
South Lebanon~5.0 – 5.5
Mount Lebanon (higher altitude)~4.6 – 5.2

⚡ Electricity prices in Lebanon

State utility (EDL) supply has collapsed to only a few hours a day in much of the country, forcing reliance on private diesel generator subscriptions billed per ampere at very high, dollarised rates. Against those costs, solar pays back unusually fast — it is competing with some of the most expensive effective electricity anywhere.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Formal net metering existed historically but has been undermined by the grid's collapse; in practice almost all systems are hybrid solar + battery for self-reliance, not grid export. Financing schemes and subsidised loans have appeared at various times through banks and international programmes — check what is currently available.

💡 Local insight: Lebanon

Lebanon is one of the fastest solar-adoption stories in the region — not for green reasons but out of necessity. When the state grid gives only a few hours a day and generator bills are dollarised and brutal, a solar-plus-battery system that runs the essentials pays for itself remarkably quickly. The key design decision here is battery sizing: enough storage to bridge the hours with no sun or grid, without over-spending on capacity a household can't afford up front.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Lebanon get?

About 4.8 to 5.6. The coast and Bekaa Valley are highest; the mountains slightly lower.

Is solar worth it in Lebanon?

Very much — with state power down to a few hours a day and costly private generators, solar-plus-battery often pays back unusually fast.

Does Lebanon have net metering?

Net metering existed historically but has been undermined by the grid crisis. In practice systems are hybrid solar + battery for self-reliance. Check current schemes.

How much does solar cost in Lebanon?

A small battery system for essentials is roughly $3,000–$6,000; a 5 kW home system with battery $6,000–$11,000. Often priced in USD. Get local quotes.

Note: Lebanon's electricity situation, generator costs and prices change rapidly. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with a local installer.
