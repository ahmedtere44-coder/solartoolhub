Solar panels in Spain

Spain is one of Europe's sunniest countries, and since surplus-energy rules were simplified, rooftop solar has boomed. High electricity prices plus strong sun make self-consumption very worthwhile, especially in the south.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Spain

Spain has excellent sun for Europe — roughly 4 to 5.5 peak sun hours. Andalusia and the south are highest; the green north lower.

⚡ Electricity prices in Spain

Spanish households pay relatively high electricity prices — often around €0.20–€0.30 per kWh depending on tariff and time of day. With strong sun, self-consuming your own solar during the day avoids those expensive units and is the core of the savings.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Spain uses a self-consumption model with surplus compensation (compensación de excedentes) that credits the energy you export on your bill, plus regional subsidies and IBI (property-tax) rebates in many municipalities. The old "sun tax" was removed, which is what unlocked the boom. Check your region's current grants and your retailer's surplus rate.

💡 Local insight: Spain

Spain rewards self-consumption: because surplus is compensated at a lower rate than you pay for grid power, the best returns come from using your own solar during the day (appliances, pool pumps, AC) rather than exporting it. In the sunny south a well-sized system often pays back in about 5–8 years, faster than most of Europe.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Spain get?

About 4 to 5.5. Andalusia and the south are highest (~5–5.5), while the northern coast (Galicia) is lower at ~3.6–4.2.

Is solar worth it in Spain?

Yes — high electricity prices plus strong sun give a typical payback of about 5–8 years, faster in the south. Self-consumption drives the savings.

Does Spain pay you for solar you export?

Yes, through surplus compensation (compensación de excedentes) credited on your bill, usually at a lower rate than you pay for grid power. Many regions add subsidies and property-tax rebates.

How much do solar panels cost in Spain?

Roughly €1,200–€1,800 per kWp, so about €4,000–€6,000 for a 3 kWp system. Get local quotes.

Note: Spanish electricity prices, surplus-compensation rates and regional subsidies change over time. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify locally.
