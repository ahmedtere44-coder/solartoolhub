Solar panels in Brazil

Brazil is the largest solar market in Latin America, with excellent sun almost everywhere and a well-established net-metering scheme. Rising electricity tariffs have made rooftop solar a popular investment for homes and businesses.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Brazil

Brazil has strong, consistent sun nationwide — roughly 4.5 to 6 peak sun hours. The north-east and central plateau are highest.

⚡ Electricity prices in Brazil

Brazilian electricity tariffs have climbed and vary by distributor and "tariff flag", commonly around R$0.70–R$1.00 per kWh for residential customers. High tariffs plus strong sun make self-generation attractive across most of the country.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Brazil has a mature net-metering scheme (compensação de energia, under ANEEL rules) that credits exported energy against your bill, with credits usable for months. A legal framework (Lei 14.300) is phasing in some charges on the grid-use portion for new systems, so the exact compensation depends on when you connect — check the current ANEEL rules and your distributor.

💡 Local insight: Brazil

Brazil's combination of strong sun everywhere and a long-standing net-metering scheme gives reliable paybacks, commonly around 4–6 years. The main thing to check is timing: newer rules (Lei 14.300) gradually add a charge on the grid portion of compensated energy, so confirm exactly how your distributor will credit exports before sizing the system.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Brazil get?

About 4.5 to 6. The north-east and central-west are highest (~5.2–6), the south-east around 4.8–5.4, and the far south a little lower.

Is solar worth it in Brazil?

Yes — strong sun plus rising tariffs and net metering give a typical payback of about 4–6 years. It's the biggest solar market in Latin America.

Does Brazil have net metering?

Yes, a mature scheme (compensação de energia under ANEEL). Newer rules (Lei 14.300) add some grid charges for new systems, so confirm current terms with your distributor.

How much does solar cost in Brazil?

Roughly R$3.50–R$5.00 per watt, so about R$11,000–R$16,000 for a 3 kWp system. Get local quotes.

Note: Brazilian electricity tariffs, net-metering rules (Lei 14.300) and prices change over time. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with ANEEL/your distributor and a local installer.
