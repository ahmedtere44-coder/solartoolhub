Real 5kW solar system example

A complete, worked example of a 5kW rooftop solar system — the exact components, how much it produces, what it costs, and how fast it pays back. Based on a real installation in Irbid, Jordan.

🔧 System at a glance

This is a mid-size grid-tied system suited to a typical family home. It uses eight high-power panels and a 5kW inverter. A battery is optional and shown separately.

ComponentSpecification
Solar panels8 × 625 W Jinko monocrystalline = 5,000 Wp (5.0 kWp)
Inverter5 kW grid-tied string inverter (ABB / FIMER in this example)
MountingRoof rails, south-facing, fixed tilt (~30° in this example)
Cabling & protectionDC & AC cable, DC isolator, breakers, surge protection, earthing
Battery (optional)~10 kWh lithium (LiFePO4) for partial backup

Size your own array with the panel calculator and match the inverter with the inverter calculator.

☀️ Estimated production

Using about 5.5 peak sun hours (a sunny climate) and a realistic performance ratio of ~0.8 for losses (heat, wiring, inverter), a 5.0 kWp array gives roughly:

PeriodApprox. production
Per day~22 kWh
Per month~660 kWh
Per year~8,000 kWh

Production is higher in summer and lower in winter. Check your own output with the energy production calculator, and find your local sun hours in peak sun hours by location.

💰 Cost breakdown (Jordan, approximate)

Grid-tied, no battery — rough planning figures for Jordan (prices move, always get local quotes):

8 × 625 W panels~800 – 1,200
5 kW inverter (ABB / FIMER class)~600 – 1,000
Mounting, cable, protection~450 – 700
Installation & wiring~500 – 800
Total (grid-tied)~2,500 – 3,800
Optional 10 kWh battery+ ~1,400 – 2,600

📈 Savings & payback

In the real example above, a family home in Irbid had its electricity bill fully covered after installing this system. A 5 kW array producing ~660 kWh a month offsets a large family bill; depending on your tariff block that is roughly 80–160 JD per month saved, so a ~2,800–3,300 JD grid-tied system typically pays back in about 2–4 years — after which the power is essentially free for the panels' 25+ year life.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many panels are in a 5kW solar system?

Usually 8 to 10 panels. This example uses 8 × 625W Jinko panels for about 5.0 kWp.

How much power does a 5kW solar system produce per day?

Around 22 kWh/day at ~5.5 peak sun hours for a 5.0 kWp array, so about 660 kWh/month and ~8,000 kWh/year.

How much does a 5kW solar system cost?

Roughly 2,500-3,800 JD grid-tied in Jordan, more with a battery. Get local quotes.

Is a 5kW solar system worth it?

For a typical family home, yes — with net metering it can pay back in roughly 2-5 years and then produce for 25+ years.

Note: all figures are approximate and for planning only. Production depends on your sun hours, shading and roof orientation; prices and tariffs vary and change over time. Always confirm with local installers and your electricity provider.
