Solar panels in Portugal

Europe's best solar resource alongside Spain — high electricity prices and a simple self-consumption regime (UPAC).

⚡ Electricity prices in Portugal

Household electricity runs roughly €0.22–0.30 per kWh depending on your supplier and time-of-use plan — among the higher EU rates.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Portugal's UPAC regime (Upgrades for Self-Consumption) removed licensing for systems under 1 MW and lets small exports be compensated. The environmental fund (Fundo Ambiental) runs periodic subsidies for residential PV-plus-battery, and VAT on panels and batteries was cut to 6%.

💡 Local insight: Portugal

Portugal's Atlantic edge is the local wildcard: coastal fog and sea salt eat uncoated frames within a few summers, so specify anodized or marine-rated aluminium near the coast. The Alentejo is Europe's sunniest large region — systems there outperform their German-style sizing by 30%. And the 6% VAT cut applies only to equipment properly invoiced under the reduced rate; owners who accept 'standard' invoices lose 19 points on every euro.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Portugal get?

Roughly 3.6 to 5.8. The Alentejo and Algarve are Europe-class (~5.2–5.8), Lisbon around 4.6–5.1, and the rainy north about 4.1–4.6.

Is solar worth it in Portugal?

Yes — high EU retail rates, 6% VAT on equipment and the simple UPAC regime bring payback to roughly 4–7 years.

Does Portugal have net metering?

Portugal uses a simplified self-consumption regime (UPAC): exports from small systems are compensated monthly, with no licensing under 1 MW.

How much does solar cost in Portugal?

Roughly €1,300–1,800 per kW installed for a typical home system.

Note: Portugal electricity tariffs, net-metering rules and prices change frequently (and with the currency). These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with your utility and a local installer.
