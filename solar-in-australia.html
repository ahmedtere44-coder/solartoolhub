Solar panels in Australia

Australia has the highest rooftop-solar uptake per person in the world — and for good reason. Strong sunshine, high electricity prices and the federal STC rebate combine to give some of the fastest solar paybacks on the planet.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Australia

Australia is very sunny. Peak sun hours range from about 3.5–4.5 in the southern cities to well over 5.5–6 in the north and centre. Even the "low" areas beat most of Europe.

⚡ Electricity prices in Australia

Australian households pay some of the higher electricity prices in the world — roughly 25–35 cents (AUD) per kWh, depending on the state and retailer. That high grid price is a big part of why self-consumed solar is so valuable here: every kWh you use from your own panels avoids that full retail rate.

💸 Incentives & net metering

The federal STC scheme (Small-scale Technology Certificates) gives an upfront discount worth thousands of dollars on a typical system — it's usually already subtracted from the quoted price. On top, a feed-in tariff pays you for exported energy, though rates are now modest (around 5–10 c/kWh) and vary by state and retailer, so it's best to size for self-consumption first.

💰 System cost & payback in Australia

After the STC rebate, Australian prices are among the lowest in the world — often around $0.90–$1.30 (AUD) per watt installed:

💡 Local insight: Australia

Because systems are so cheap after the STC rebate and grid power is expensive, Australian paybacks are often just 3–6 years — among the fastest anywhere. The key is to size for self-consumption (use your own power during the day) since feed-in tariffs are now low; adding a battery or shifting big loads to daytime boosts the return.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Australia get?

From about 3.5–4.5 in Melbourne and the south, up to 5.5–6.5 in the north and centre (Darwin, Alice Springs). Sydney is around 4–4.5 and Perth/Adelaide about 4.8–5.5.

Is solar worth it in Australia?

Very much so. Cheap systems (after the STC rebate) plus high electricity prices give some of the world's fastest paybacks, commonly 3–6 years.

What is the STC rebate?

The Small-scale Technology Certificate scheme is a federal incentive that discounts the upfront cost of a solar system by thousands of dollars — normally already included in the price you're quoted.

How much does a solar system cost in Australia?

A popular 6.6 kW system is often around $5,000–$9,000 AUD after the STC rebate, working out to roughly $0.90–$1.30 per watt.

Note: Australian electricity prices, the STC rebate value and state feed-in tariffs change over time. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with your retailer and a local installer.
