Solar panels in Pakistan

Pakistan has become one of the world's fastest-growing solar markets. Strong sunshine, rising grid tariffs and frequent load-shedding have pushed huge numbers of homes and businesses to rooftop solar, often with net metering.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Pakistan

Pakistan is very sunny — roughly 5 to 6 peak sun hours. The south and Balochistan are highest; the north a little lower.

⚡ Electricity prices in Pakistan

Grid electricity tariffs have risen sharply and are now high for many households — commonly Rs 30–60+ per unit (kWh) in the upper slabs, plus taxes and surcharges. That, combined with load-shedding, is why solar has boomed: every self-generated unit avoids an expensive and unreliable grid unit.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Most areas offer net metering (through DISCOs and NEPRA rules) that credits exported energy, and there is no GST on many solar components. Terms — especially the net-metering buy-back rate — have been under review and vary, so confirm the current policy with your DISCO before sizing an export system.

💡 Local insight: Pakistan

Pakistan's mix of high tariffs, load-shedding and strong sun gives some of the fastest paybacks in the region — often around 3–5 years for a grid-tied system with net metering. Because outages are common, many buyers add a battery or hybrid inverter for backup; if pure savings are the goal, size for daytime self-consumption and net metering first.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Pakistan get?

About 5 to 6. The south and Balochistan are highest (~5.7–6.2), Punjab around 5–5.6, and the north a little lower.

Is solar worth it in Pakistan?

Very much so — high tariffs, load-shedding and strong sun give fast paybacks, commonly 3–5 years with net metering.

Does Pakistan have net metering?

Yes, through the DISCOs under NEPRA rules, crediting exported energy. Buy-back terms have changed over time, so confirm the current policy locally.

How much does solar cost in Pakistan?

A grid-tied 3–5 kW system is roughly PKR 500,000–900,000; adding a battery for load-shedding raises it. Get local quotes.

Note: Pakistan electricity tariffs, net-metering buy-back rates and prices change frequently. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with your DISCO and a local installer.
