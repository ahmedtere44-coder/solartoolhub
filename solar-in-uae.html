Solar panels in the UAE 🇦🇪

The UAE combines strong sunshine with an ambitious clean-energy push — from the Mohammed bin Rashid Solar Park to the Shams Dubai rooftop scheme. High air-conditioning use also means big bills that solar can offset.

☀️ Peak sun hours in the UAE

The UAE is very sunny — roughly 5.5 to 6.5 peak sun hours, with the desert interior highest and some coastal haze slightly reducing output near the coast.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Al Ain & the interior~6.0 – 6.5
Abu Dhabi~5.8 – 6.3
Dubai & Sharjah~5.6 – 6.1
Northern Emirates (RAK, Fujairah)~5.5 – 6.0

Use your local figure in the solar panel calculator and the energy production calculator. Background: peak sun hours by location.

⚡ Electricity prices in the UAE

Residential electricity (for example DEWA in Dubai) runs on slabs, roughly AED 0.23–0.38 per kWh depending on consumption, plus fuel surcharges. Heavy summer air-conditioning pushes many homes into the higher slabs — exactly where solar self-generation saves the most.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Dubai's Shams Dubai scheme lets buildings install rooftop solar and export surplus to DEWA on a net-metering basis; Sharjah (SEWA) and Abu Dhabi have their own programmes. There's no cash rebate as such, but net metering plus high consumption makes the economics work. Confirm the current rules with your local utility.

💡 Local insight: UAE

In the UAE the summer air-conditioning load is the key. A home that runs heavy AC spends much of the year in the higher tariff slabs, so a rooftop system sized to cover daytime cooling can save a lot and pay back in roughly 6–10 years. Shams Dubai net metering means surplus in milder months is credited rather than wasted.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does the UAE get?

About 5.5 to 6.5, with Al Ain and the interior highest and coastal areas slightly lower due to haze.

Is solar worth it in the UAE?

Yes for homes with heavy air-conditioning, since high summer use pushes you into the top tariff slabs where solar saves the most. Payback is typically around 6–10 years.

What is Shams Dubai?

Dubai's rooftop solar net-metering scheme (through DEWA) that lets buildings generate their own power and export surplus for credit. Sharjah and Abu Dhabi run similar programmes.

How much does solar cost in the UAE?

Roughly AED 18,000–30,000 for a 5 kW villa system, scaling up for larger homes and higher AC loads. Get local quotes.

Note: UAE tariffs (DEWA/SEWA/others) and net-metering rules change over time and differ by emirate. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with your utility and a local installer.
