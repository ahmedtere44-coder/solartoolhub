Solar panels in France

France enjoys a real north-south sunshine split, from the bright Mediterranean to the cooler north. A self-consumption premium plus a feed-in obligation for surplus energy make rooftop solar a solid long-term investment, especially in the south.

☀️ Peak sun hours in France

France's sun varies clearly by region — roughly 3 to 4.5 peak sun hours. Provence and the Mediterranean are best; the north lower.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Provence & the Mediterranean~4.0 – 4.5
South-West & Rhône valley~3.7 – 4.2
Central France~3.3 – 3.8
Paris & the north~3.0 – 3.5
Brittany & the north-west~2.9 – 3.4

⚡ Electricity prices in France

French residential electricity runs on the regulated tariff at roughly €0.23 to €0.27 per kWh. It's not the highest in Europe, but combined with self-consumption incentives it makes rooftop solar worthwhile — most of all in the sunnier south.

💸 Incentives & net metering

France supports rooftop solar with a self-consumption premium (prime à l'autoconsommation) paid over the first years, plus a feed-in obligation (obligation d'achat) that buys your surplus at a set rate, and reduced VAT on smaller systems. Amounts are revised each quarter, so check the current figures before installing.

💡 Local insight: France

In France the north-south split really matters: a south-facing roof in Provence can generate close to 50% more than the same roof in Brittany, so payback in the sunny south is noticeably faster. With the self-consumption premium and the feed-in obligation for surplus, a well-sized system typically pays back in about 9–13 years, sooner in the south.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does France get?

About 3 to 4.5. Provence and the Mediterranean are highest (~4–4.5), while Paris and the north-west are around 3–3.5.

Is solar worth it in France?

Yes, especially in the sunnier south. The self-consumption premium plus the feed-in obligation give a typical payback of about 9–13 years, faster in the Mediterranean regions.

What incentives does France offer for solar?

A self-consumption premium (prime à l'autoconsommation), a feed-in obligation (obligation d'achat) that buys your surplus, and reduced VAT on smaller systems. Rates are revised quarterly.

How much do solar panels cost in France?

Roughly €2,000–€2,500 per kWp, so about €6,000–€8,000 for a 3 kWp system. Get local quotes.

Note: French electricity prices, the self-consumption premium and feed-in rates change (revised quarterly). These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify current rates and use a certified (RGE) installer.
