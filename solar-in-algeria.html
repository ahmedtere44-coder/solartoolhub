Solar panels in Algeria

Algeria has some of the highest solar potential in the world thanks to its vast Sahara, though heavily subsidised electricity means the payback maths is different from most countries. For remote and off-grid sites especially, solar is compelling.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Algeria

Algeria has enormous sun, especially in the Sahara — roughly 5.5 to 7 peak sun hours. The desert south is exceptional; the Mediterranean north a little lower.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Sahara (Tamanrasset, Adrar)~6.5 – 7.0
High Plateaus~5.8 – 6.4
Interior (Ghardaia)~6.0 – 6.6
Mediterranean coast (Algiers)~5.0 – 5.6

Use your local figure in the solar panel calculator and the energy production calculator. Background: peak sun hours by location.

⚡ Electricity prices in Algeria

Electricity in Algeria is heavily subsidised and very cheap by international standards (a low fraction of a US dollar per kWh via Sonelgaz). Cheap power means grid-tied solar saves less money per kWh, so the payback is slower than in high-tariff countries — the strongest case is for remote sites, water pumping and off-grid needs.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Algeria has national renewable-energy targets and programmes, historically focused on utility-scale desert projects rather than residential rooftops. Formal residential net metering is limited, so most private systems are for self-consumption or off-grid use. Check for current national programmes and any support before installing.

💡 Local insight: Algeria

Algeria is a paradox: world-class sun but very cheap subsidised grid power, so a purely financial rooftop payback in a connected city home is slow. Where solar really shines here is off-grid and productive uses — desert farms, water pumping and remote sites far from the grid — where the enormous irradiance and the cost of alternatives (diesel, grid extension) make it the obvious choice.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Algeria get?

About 5.5 to 7 — among the highest in the world. The Sahara reaches ~6.5–7, while the Mediterranean coast is around 5–5.6.

Is solar worth it in Algeria?

The sun resource is exceptional, but heavily subsidised cheap electricity slows the grid-tied payback. Solar is most worthwhile for off-grid sites, farms and water pumping.

Does Algeria have net metering?

Formal residential net metering is limited; national programmes have focused on large desert projects. Most private systems are for self-consumption or off-grid. Confirm current rules locally.

How much does solar cost in Algeria?

Roughly $3,200–$5,500 for a 5 kW system. Off-grid systems cost more once batteries are added. Get local quotes.

Note: Algerian electricity tariffs, programmes and prices change over time. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with Sonelgaz and a local installer.
