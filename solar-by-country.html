Why solar differs by country

Three things drive whether solar pays off, and all three are local: peak sun hours (how much energy each panel makes), the electricity price you avoid by self-generating, and the incentives and net-metering rules where you live. A sunny country with expensive power and good net metering — like Jordan or Australia — can pay back a system in a handful of years, while a cloudy country with cheap power takes longer.

Wherever you are, size your system with the panel calculator, check the return with the ROI calculator, or design everything at once with the system report builder.
