Solar panels in Bangladesh

Bangladesh runs one of the world's most successful off-grid solar programmes, having brought power to millions of rural homes through solar home systems. Now grid-tied rooftop solar and net metering are growing in its cities too.

☀️ Peak sun hours in Bangladesh

Bangladesh gets solid tropical sun — roughly 4.2 to 5 peak sun hours — highest in the dry season and in the west, lower during the monsoon.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Western Bangladesh (Rajshahi)~4.7 – 5.0
Dhaka & central~4.4 – 4.8
Chittagong & south-east~4.3 – 4.7
Monsoon-season average~3.5 – 4.2

Use your local figure in the solar panel calculator and the energy production calculator. Background: peak sun hours by location.

⚡ Electricity prices in Bangladesh

Grid electricity is billed in rising slabs, relatively affordable at the bottom but climbing for higher use — commonly the equivalent of $0.05–$0.10 per kWh across residential slabs. Higher-consumption homes and businesses gain most from rooftop self-generation.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Bangladesh has a net-metering policy (via SREDA and the distribution utilities) for rooftop systems, allowing export credit, and a globally celebrated solar home system (SHS) programme that electrified millions of off-grid rural households. Confirm current net-metering terms with your utility before sizing an export system.

💡 Local insight: Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a genuine solar success story: its solar home system programme is one of the largest off-grid electrification efforts ever, reaching millions of rural families with small panel-and-battery kits. In the cities, the story is now shifting to grid-tied rooftops with net metering. Monsoon season noticeably cuts output, so honest production estimates should use dry-season and wet-season figures rather than a single annual average.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does Bangladesh get?

About 4.2 to 5, higher in the dry season and the west, and lower during the monsoon (~3.5–4.2).

Is solar worth it in Bangladesh?

Yes — off-grid solar home systems are transformative for rural areas, and grid-tied rooftops with net metering increasingly pay off for higher-consumption urban homes and businesses.

Does Bangladesh have net metering?

Yes, there is a net-metering policy via SREDA and the utilities for rooftop systems. Confirm current terms with your distribution utility.

How much does solar cost in Bangladesh?

Small solar home systems can cost a few hundred dollars; a grid-tied 3 kW rooftop is roughly $1,800–$3,200. Get local quotes.

Note: Bangladeshi tariffs, net-metering rules and prices change over time. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with SREDA / your utility and a local installer.
