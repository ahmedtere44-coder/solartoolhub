Solar panels in South Africa

South Africa is one of the sunniest countries in the world, and with Eskom tariffs climbing and load-shedding a fact of life, solar (often with a battery) has become as much about keeping the lights on as about saving money.

☀️ Peak sun hours in South Africa

South Africa gets excellent sun — roughly 5 to 6.5 peak sun hours. The interior and Northern Cape are highest; the coastal cities a little lower.

RegionApprox. peak sun hours (annual avg)
Northern Cape & interior~6.0 – 6.5
Free State & Gauteng~5.3 – 5.8
Eastern & Western interior~5.0 – 5.6
Durban & KwaZulu-Natal coast~4.8 – 5.3
Cape Town & southern coast~4.5 – 5.3

Use your local figure in the solar panel calculator and the energy production calculator. Background: peak sun hours by location.

⚡ Electricity prices in South Africa

Eskom residential tariffs have risen steeply and are now roughly R2.50–R4.00 per kWh depending on your municipality and tariff plan. Combined with load-shedding, that rising price is the main reason rooftop solar has taken off — every self-generated kWh avoids both the cost and the blackout.

💸 Incentives & net metering

Support has varied: a temporary rooftop rebate for individuals ran in 2023–2024, and businesses can use Section 12B allowances. Net metering / feed-in depends on your municipality (for example, the City of Cape Town has a feed-in scheme) — many still cap or restrict residential export, so most homes size for self-consumption plus a battery for backup. Confirm your municipality's current rules.

💰 System cost & payback in South Africa

Prices vary with whether you add a battery (very common here for load-shedding). As a rough guide:

💡 Local insight: South Africa

In South Africa the maths is different from most countries: because of load-shedding, many people buy solar-plus-battery for reliability first and savings second. If your main goal is backup, size the battery for your essential loads during typical blackout windows; if it's pure savings, a grid-tied system sized for daytime self-consumption pays back fastest as Eskom prices keep rising.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How many peak sun hours does South Africa get?

About 5 to 6.5. The Northern Cape and interior are highest (~6–6.5), while coastal Cape Town and Durban are around 4.5–5.3.

Is solar worth it in South Africa?

For most homes yes — rising Eskom tariffs plus load-shedding make solar attractive both for savings and for keeping power during blackouts. Payback is commonly around 5–8 years for grid-tied systems.

Does solar help with load-shedding?

Only if you add a battery (or a hybrid inverter with backup). A grid-tied-only system shuts down during a blackout for safety; a battery system keeps your essential loads running.

How much does solar cost in South Africa?

A grid-tied 3–5 kW system is roughly R60,000–110,000; adding a battery for load-shedding backup pushes a 5 kW system to about R110,000–180,000. Get local quotes.

Note: Eskom tariffs, municipal net-metering rules and prices in South Africa change frequently. These figures are approximate, for planning only — verify with your municipality and a local installer.
